\section*{Per-Metric Efficiency Verification}
Table~\ref{tab:per-metric-shapley-verification} presents
the per-metric efficiency of the Shapley layer for evidence-first versus claims-first candidates, Jordan Smith and Felix Vance respectively.
As we establish in Section~\ref{subsec:study-11-shapley-verification}, the efficiency
axiom states that the sum of the source attributions must be perfectly equal to the final evaluated score. These breakdowns
show that the axiom holds not only for the candidate's global score, but also for every individual granular metric provided by MERIT.
